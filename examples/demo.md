# 演示：数据中心检修配图

## 输入

## 提示词示例

**画面重点：** 通过工程师的实际检修动作表现数据中心的工作现场，人物与设备环境都清楚可见。

**中文提示词：**

```text
法新社风格新闻纪实摄影，AI生成示意图。一名工程师正在数据中心机房检查服务器设备，专注观察机柜，人物处于自然工作状态，不看镜头、不摆拍。中景环境构图，人物是画面主体，前景可见少量线缆和设备细节，中景呈现服务器机柜，背景交代机房空间。自然、克制的室内照明，真实肤色与设备材质，适度低饱和的自然色彩，35mm新闻摄影镜头感，现场观察视角，保留真实环境细节。不要科幻霓虹、夸张光效、广告式精修、可读的虚构数据、品牌标志、AFP 标志或水印。
```

**English prompt:**

```text
AFP photojournalism style, AI-generated editorial illustration. An engineer is inspecting server equipment in a data-center room, focused on the cabinets. Show a natural working moment; the subject does not pose or look at the camera. Use an environmental medium shot: the engineer is the main subject, a few cables and equipment details appear in the foreground, server racks occupy the middle ground, and the room provides context in the background. Natural, restrained indoor lighting, realistic skin tones and materials, moderately muted natural colors, a 35mm news-photography feel, an observed on-site perspective, and credible environmental details. No science-fiction neon, dramatic effects, advertising retouching, invented readable screen data, brand marks, AFP logos, or watermarks.
```

> 这是提示词示例，不是实际新闻照片，也没有记录真实事件。
